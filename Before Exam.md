# Exam Performance Notes

## Time Management
- **Current pace:** 1 question per 3 minutes
- **Target pace:** 1 question per 2 minutes
- **Strategy:** Breathe before every question to properly reset from previous question and clear mind more the upcoming one

## Causes of Slow Pace

**Slow Start**
- Takes time to get into the zone
- **Solution:** Warm up with sample questions before exam
- **Alternative:** Review flashcards before starting

**Focus Issues**
- Spacing out during exam
- **Solution:** Increase meditation sessions

**Mental Stamina**
- Fatigue after ~25 questions
- Need to build endurance
	- How? Just Practice BRO 🙃
